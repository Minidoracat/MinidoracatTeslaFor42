description=[h2]📦 Requirements[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] Works in singleplayer and multiplayer; in multiplayer the server must enable this mod
description=[*] [b]Optional:[/b] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3813972279]Minidoracat Vehicle Manager[/url] (vehicle claims; the phone app and the keys follow its permissions)
description=[*] [b]Optional:[/b] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3792675881]Minidoracat MiniMap - AutoDrive[/url] (Summon in the phone app)
description=[*] [b]Optional:[/b] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3763913359]Minidoracat MiniMap[/url] (your Teslas on the minimap, navigate to your car)
description=[*] [b]Add/remove mid-save:[/b] can be added; read the full guide before removing it: the Teslas, their keys and the chargers disappear or stop working
description=[*] [b]Languages:[/b] 繁體中文, 简体中文, English, 日本語, 한국어, Русский, Español, Português, Türkçe, Français, Polski, Deutsch (please report translation issues)
description=[/list]

description=[h2]🚀 Quick start[/h2]
description=[olist]
description=[*] Find a Tesla in a parking lot; its car key turns into a key card when you pick it up
description=[*] Sit in the driver's seat and click the power button at the top left of the dashboard
description=[*] With Electrical 3, make a Wall Connector, mount it on a powered wall and right-click it to plug in the charge cable
description=[*] Sit in the car with the key card, right-click a smartphone and choose Set Up as Phone Key to use the Tesla app
description=[/olist]
description=
description=[h2]✨ Features[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Two Performance cars[/b]: Model Y and Model 3, 8 factory paints each
description=[*] [b]Battery and charging[/b]: driving uses charge, braking wins some back, charge at a Wall Connector
description=[*] [b]Tesla-style dashboard[/b]: speed, charge and range; click the car picture to open its doors
description=[*] [b]Key card and phone key[/b]: unlocks as you walk up with your phone, locks as you walk away
description=[*] [b]Tesla app[/b]: lock, find the car and watch it charge from afar; one phone for several cars
description=[*] [b]Sentry and Camp Mode[/b]: get told when someone touches the car, keep the climate on while camping
description=[*] [b]Summon[/b]: with AutoDrive and its autopilot module, call the empty car to you from the app
description=[*] [b]Vehicle Manager and minimap[/b]: its permissions apply, and the minimap shows your cars
description=[/list]
