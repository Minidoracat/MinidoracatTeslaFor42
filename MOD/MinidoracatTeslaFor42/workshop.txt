description=[h2]🚀 Quick start[/h2]
description=[olist]
description=[*] Find a Tesla in a parking lot or an upscale neighbourhood (the Model S, Model X and Cybertruck only in upscale ones); its car key turns into a key card when you pick it up
description=[*] Sit in the driver's seat and click the power button at the top left of the dashboard
description=[*] With Electrical 3, make a Wall Connector and mount it on a powered wall, or set a Mobile Connector on the floor; right-click it to plug in the charge cable
description=[*] Find a smartphone (a new item from this mod, most common in electronics stores; a Tesla's glove box may hold one too), sit in the car with the key card, right-click the phone and choose Set Up as Phone Key to use the Tesla app
description=[/olist]
description=
description=[h2]✨ Features[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Five top-trim Teslas[/b]: Model Y and Model 3 Performance, Model S and Model X Plaid, Cybertruck Cyberbeast
description=[*] [b]Model X and Cybertruck[/b]: falcon wing doors and six seats, a cargo bed, Armor Glass and Powershare home backup
description=[*] [b]Battery and charging[/b]: driving uses charge, braking wins some back, charge at a Wall Connector or a Mobile Connector on the floor
description=[*] [b]Tesla-style dashboard[/b]: speed, charge and range; click the car picture to open its doors
description=[*] [b]Phone key and Tesla app[/b]: unlocks as you walk up, locks as you walk away; lock, find the car and watch it charge from afar
description=[*] [b]Sentry and Camp Mode[/b]: get told when someone touches, breaks into or tows the car, keep the climate on while camping
description=[*] [b]Bioweapon Defense Mode and Chill[/b]: the HEPA filter keeps corpse sickness out, and acceleration can go gentle
description=[*] [b]Family mods[/b]: AutoDrive's FSD view and Summon, Vehicle Manager permissions, your cars on the minimap
