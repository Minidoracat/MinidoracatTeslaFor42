description=[h2]🚀 Quick start[/h2]
description=[olist]
description=[*] Find a Tesla in a parking lot (the Cybertruck in upscale neighbourhoods); its car key turns into a key card when you pick it up
description=[*] Sit in the driver's seat and click the power button at the top left of the dashboard
description=[*] With Electrical 3, make a Wall Connector and mount it on a powered wall, or set a Mobile Connector on the floor; right-click it to plug in the charge cable
description=[*] Find a smartphone (a new item from this mod, most common in electronics stores; a Tesla's glove box may hold one too), sit in the car with the key card, right-click the phone and choose Set Up as Phone Key to use the Tesla app
description=[/olist]
description=
description=[h2]✨ Features[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Three top-trim Teslas[/b]: Model Y and Model 3 Performance with 8 factory paints each, Cybertruck Cyberbeast with 12
description=[*] [b]Cybertruck[/b]: cargo bed with a powered tonneau cover, Armor Glass, roof light bar; Powershare runs your home in a blackout, and the bed outlets charge another Tesla
description=[*] [b]Battery and charging[/b]: driving uses charge, braking wins some back, charge at a Wall Connector or a Mobile Connector on the floor
description=[*] [b]Tesla-style dashboard[/b]: speed, charge and range; click the car picture to open its doors
description=[*] [b]Key card and phone key[/b]: unlocks as you walk up with your phone, locks as you walk away
description=[*] [b]Tesla app[/b]: lock, find the car and watch it charge from afar; one phone for several cars
description=[*] [b]Sentry and Camp Mode[/b]: get told when someone touches the car, keep the climate on while camping
description=[*] [b]FSD view and Summon[/b]: with AutoDrive, watch the route, cars and zombies while it drives itself, or call the car over from the app
description=[*] [b]Vehicle Manager and minimap[/b]: its permissions apply, and the minimap shows your cars
description=[/list]
