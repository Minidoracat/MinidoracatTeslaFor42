[h1]⚡ Minidoracat Tesla Collection[/h1]
[h3]By Minidoracat[/h3]

[hr][/hr]

Drive a Tesla Model S, Model 3, Model X, Model Y, Model Y L or Cybertruck through the apocalypse: run on battery power, charge at home or at a gas station's Supercharger, and control the car from the Tesla app on your phone. More models and charging equipment will be added over time.

[h2]📦 Requirements[/h2]
[list]
[*] Works in singleplayer and multiplayer; in multiplayer the server must enable this mod
[*] [b]Optional:[/b] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3813972279]Minidoracat Vehicle Manager[/url] (vehicle claims; the phone app and the keys follow its permissions)
[*] [b]Optional:[/b] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3792675881]Minidoracat MiniMap - AutoDrive[/url] (Summon in the phone app)
[*] [b]Optional:[/b] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3763913359]Minidoracat MiniMap[/url] (your Teslas on the minimap, navigate to your car)
[*] [b]Add/remove mid-save:[/b] can be added; read the full guide before removing it: each Tesla turns into a random other vehicle, keys and charger items vanish, and placed chargers become blank objects; turn off a Cybertruck's Powershare first
[*] [b]Languages:[/b] 繁體中文, 简体中文, English, 日本語, 한국어, Русский, Español, Português, Türkçe, Français, Polski, Deutsch (please report translation issues)
[/list]

[h2]🚀 Quick start[/h2]
[olist]
[*] Find a Tesla in a parking lot or an upscale neighbourhood (the Model S, Model X and Cybertruck only in upscale ones; the Model Y L in both); its car key turns into a key card when you pick it up
[*] Sit in the driver's seat and click the power button at the top left of the dashboard
[*] With Electrical 3, make a Wall Connector and mount it on a powered wall, set a Mobile Connector on the floor, or find a Supercharger at a gas station; right-click it to plug in the charge cable
[*] Find a smartphone (a new item from this mod, most common in electronics stores; a Tesla's glove box may hold one too), sit in the car with the key card, right-click the phone and choose Set Up as Phone Key to use the Tesla app
[/olist]

[h2]✨ Features[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Six Teslas[/b]: Model Y, Model Y L, Model 3 Performance, Model S and Model X Plaid, Cybertruck Cyberbeast
[*] [b]Six seats and Cybertruck[/b]: Model X falcon wing doors, Model Y L six seats; a cargo bed, Armor Glass and Powershare home backup
[*] [b]Battery and charging[/b]: braking wins charge back; charge at a Wall Connector, a Mobile Connector or a gas station's Supercharger
[*] [b]Tesla-style dashboard[/b]: speed, charge and range; click the car picture to open its doors
[*] [b]Phone key and Tesla app[/b]: unlocks as you walk up, locks as you walk away; lock, find the car and watch it charge from afar
[*] [b]Sentry and Camp Mode[/b]: get told when someone touches, breaks into or tows the car, keep the climate on while camping
[*] [b]Bioweapon Defense Mode and Chill[/b]: the HEPA filter keeps corpse sickness out, and acceleration can go gentle
[*] [b]Family mods[/b]: AutoDrive's FSD view and Summon, Vehicle Manager permissions, your cars on the minimap
[/list]
